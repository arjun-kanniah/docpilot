AURORA FOR SCHOOLS: PROGRAM GUIDE, 2026-27 SCHOOL YEAR
Effective 1 September 2026. Replaces the education discount terms in earlier Aurora documents.

Aurora Instruments is a fictional company created for demonstration and testing. All names,
figures and products are invented.


1. WHO CAN JOIN

Accredited K-12 schools, colleges and universities, and registered non-profit organizations.
To join, email education@aurorainstruments.example with proof of eligibility (an accreditation
letter or a non-profit registration certificate). Approval takes up to 3 business days.


2. DISCOUNTS

Verified schools and non-profit organizations receive:

- 40% off AuroraLive Plus and Pro plans, monthly or annual.
- 40% off hardware for orders of 3 or more stations. (Until 31 August 2026 this applied only
  to orders of 5 or more.)
- 15% off hardware for orders of 1 or 2 stations.

Discounts apply to the current list prices and cannot be combined with other promotions.


3. STATION GRANT FOR LOW-INCOME SCHOOLS

Title I schools, and schools outside the US with a comparable low-income designation, may apply
for the Station Grant: up to 2 free SkyNode Lite stations per school per school year. Grant
applications open on 1 February each year and are decided by 31 March. A school may receive a
grant and also buy further stations at the education discount.


4. CLASSROOM EXTRAS

- Teacher dashboard: included free with a Pro plan for verified schools. A teacher can follow
  up to 30 student accounts and share live readings with a class.
- Curriculum: 12 free lesson plans for grades 5 to 12, covering pressure systems, the water
  cycle, air quality and data analysis.
- Data export for student projects needs CSV export, which is part of the Pro plan.


5. QUESTIONS

Mountain Time.
