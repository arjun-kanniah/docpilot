# Aurora Instruments: Pricing Update

**Effective 1 September 2026.** This notice supersedes the prices and plan limits in the
Public Knowledge Base v3.2 (2026). Anything not listed here is unchanged.

*Aurora Instruments is a fictional company created for demonstration and testing. All names,
figures and products are invented.*

## Hardware prices (USD)

| Model | Price before 1 Sep 2026 | Price from 1 Sep 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| SkyNode Lite | $149 | $149 (unchanged) |
| SkyNode Pro | $299 | $319 |
| SkyNode Max | $499 | $529 |

The increases reflect higher component costs for the cellular module (Max) and the PM2.5
air-quality sensor (Pro and Max).

## AuroraLive cloud plans

| Plan | Monthly before | Monthly now | Annual before | Annual now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $0 | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Plus | $4.99 | $5.99 | $49 | $59 |
| Pro | $12.99 | $12.99 (unchanged) | $129 | $129 (unchanged) |

Plan limits also change:

- **Basic** keeps 14 days of data history, up from 7 days.
- **Plus** now allows **4 stations per account**, up from 3.
- **Pro** is unchanged: unlimited history and up to 10 stations per account.

Paying annually still saves compared with paying monthly, and every paid plan keeps its 14-day
free trial.

## Existing customers

- Hardware orders placed before 1 September 2026 are fulfilled at the earlier prices.
- Annual subscriptions renew at the new price from their first renewal after 1 September 2026.
  Monthly subscriptions change from the first bill after 1 October 2026.

## Education discount

The education discount on Plus and Pro plans stays at 40%. The hardware discount rules have
changed for the 2026-27 school year; see the Aurora for Schools Program Guide.
